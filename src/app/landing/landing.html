Onboard Company

Register your company portal. Submit profile parameters to request activation from the Super Admin approval queue.

02

Employee ESS & GPS

Employees log in to swipe in/out with coordinates check, regularization applications, holiday calendar verification, and shift schedules.

03

StaffHub HRMS Features

Everything you need to manage your HR records, attendance, payroll, and employee processes

Employee Records & Profiles

One accessible place to store contact data, documents, assigned assets, emergency details, and department hierarchies.

Attendance Swipe & Live GPS Geofencing

Real-time swipe in/out with live GPS location tracking, IP address validation, and automatic 4:00 AM swipe-out protection.

Attendance Regularization Requests

Apply for regularization for missing swipes or late marks with GPS verification, remark explanations, and manager approval queues.

Leave Requests, Accruals & Approvals

Auto-credit 1.0 EL & 0.5 CL monthly balances. Submit leave applications with half-day/full-day options and track team attendance calendars.

Probation Reviews & Confirmation Tracker

Monitor 6-month employee probation evaluations, mid-term performance appraisals, manager ratings, and confirmation status decisions.

Expense Claims & Receipt Attachments

Submit expense reimbursement claims with official travel location, dates, bill photos, itemized breakdown, and HR approval workflows.

Support Ticket Helpdesk

Raise and track support tickets for Administration, HR-CRG, and IT Helpdesk. CC team members and collaborate via comment timelines.

Task Management & Kanban Boards

Assign and collaborate on team tasks with Kanban board & Table views, progress sliders, due dates, and real-time status updates.

Payroll Processing & Salary Slips

Automated monthly salary calculation based on verified attendance, deductions, allowances, and downloadable employee PDF salary slips.

Resignation & Exit Interview Management

Submit digital resignation applications, track notice period countdowns, conduct exit interview surveys, and handle offboarding clearance.
